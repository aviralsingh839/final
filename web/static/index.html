Send data from your watch

Three ways in. Any of them works; they all land in the same local store.

  1. Bluetooth — press Pair watch above. Uses the standard Heart Rate service (0x180D). Works in Chrome on Android and desktop.
  2. HTTP push — from Wear OS, Tasker, Gadgetbridge or any script: 
    curl -X POST http://<this-device-ip>:8000/api/watch/ingest \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{"hr_bpm":72,"steps":4300,"spo2_pct":97,"sleep_hours":7.2,"device":"My Watch"}'
  3. Demo watch — clearly labelled synthetic stream, so the app is usable with no hardware.

How this section works

Diagnosis in adults requires two of three features, after other causes are excluded. Each one is reported as PRESENT, ABSENT or UNKNOWN — an unmeasured item is never counted as a "no". In adolescents, both hyperandrogenism and ovulatory dysfunction are required, and ultrasound and AMH are not used.

Watch data contributes cycle-awareness and supportive context. It cannot establish any of the three criteria.
